# Changelog

## 0.7.2
- Fixed a server crash ("Modifier is already applied on this attribute") when Mythic Upgrades' Jade aura re-applied, e.g. on an Alex's Mobs Mimicube copying Jade-infused armor.

## 0.7.1
- Mod icon (shown in Mod Menu).

## 0.7.0
- Config screen in Mod Menu (needs Cloth Config) for the Mythic particle amounts; applies right away in singleplayer.

## 0.6.0
- New config `config/rpg_gear_upgrades.json` to turn down Mythic Upgrades' gem effect particles, per gem or all at once with `override`.

## 0.5.0
- Arrows and bolts from infused bows and crossbows trigger the gem's on-hit effect (fully drawn bows; crossbow bolts always).
- Spells trigger the gem's on-hit effect only on their direct target, at most once per target per second (splash, area and beam ticks no longer proc).

## 0.4.1
- Forged and infused armor gets a two-tone look: the forge's colours on the dark shades, the gem's on the light ones.

## 0.4.0
- Renamed to RPG Gear Upgrades. Mythic Upgrades is now optional.
- Mythic gems also infuse the RPG Series' Ruby, Aeternium and Crystal weapons.
- New Ruby (Better Nether) and Aeternium (Better End) forge tiers for Netherite class armor.
- Forged armor can also be infused; bonuses stack and the gem's colours win.

## 0.3.0
- Infused armor gets +25% on its own class attributes and +1 armor toughness.
- With Jewelry installed, infused gear accepts one extra Socket Mount.

## 0.1.1
- Support Fabric Loader 0.19.5.

## 0.1.0
- Initial release: infuse Netherite RPG Series armor and weapons with Mythic Upgrades gems.
